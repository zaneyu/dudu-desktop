# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The primary user is the owner's girlfriend, a non-technical Windows 11 user who wants a gentle desktop companion without having to manage software complexity. The owner configures private pairing and installation. Daily moments are small: glance at Dudu, pet it, share a drink or a meal, take a tiny hug or a breath together, check the time in the UK, read a partner note, or adjust one setting.

## Product Purpose

Dudu Desktop Companion is a cute companion first: an affectionate animated desktop pet, a few affection interactions (pet, drink water, eat together, study together, sing for me, tiny hug, breathe with me), the UK partner clock, and end-to-end encrypted love notes from her partner. It is deliberately not a productivity tool — there are no reminders, tasks, focus timer, countdowns, mood check-ins, local note jar, quiet hours, outfits, or global hotkey. Success means setup takes under two minutes (name, look, pairing), ambient behavior never becomes disruptive, private content stays private, and every function remains understandable from standard Windows controls.

The settings window has exactly three destinations: **Home** (big Dudu, greeting, UK clock, the cute buttons, breathe with me and its stop button), **Love Notes** (pending partner notes to reveal, and the opened notes to re-read or delete), and **Settings** (look and motion, sounds, pet size and placement, launch at sign-in, partner connection, and one "delete my data"). The tray offers show/hide, pause 1 hour or resume, open Dudu, and exit; the tray pause is the only way to silence Dudu.

## Brand Personality

Warm, calm, considerate. Dudu may be playful and expressive, while the application shell remains trustworthy, restrained, and native to Windows 11.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a themed web dashboard, a dense productivity suite, a childish mobile game, or a glass-heavy AI-generated concept. Avoid decorative gradients, oversized cards, novelty controls, excessive animation, and romantic copy that feels performative or embarrassing.

## Design Principles

1. Affection without interruption: ambient moments are capped at a few a day and yield to fullscreen work, a meal or drag in progress, and the tray pause.
2. Native familiarity: use standard Windows navigation, controls, focus behavior, keyboard access, and system materials.
3. Private by default: explain local storage and encrypted pairing plainly, without exposing technical machinery in ordinary flows. Partner notes arrive encrypted; once she opens one it is kept on this PC (and in the automatic pre-migration backups) so she can re-read it, until she deletes it from the opened notes or deletes her data, and the app says so. Backups are automatic only; there is no manual backup or restore.
4. One clear next action: onboarding and empty states should be understandable at a glance.
5. Dudu carries the delight: keep the shell visually quiet so the pet remains the memorable element.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet Windows accessibility conventions with keyboard navigation, stable automation IDs, high-contrast resources, visible focus indicators, non-color status cues, readable scaling, and reduced-motion behavior available from onboarding onward.
